Privacy Policy

Your clipboard belongs on your devices.

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy covers the Carryover iPhone, iPad and desktop apps, and this website. Carryover is developed by Cole Determan. Contact determantechhelp@gmail.com with privacy questions.

What the apps do with your data

Carryover shares text and images between devices you pair together on a local network. Clipboard contents, device names, device identifiers, local connection addresses and connection status are processed on your devices to make syncing work. Clipboard transfers are encrypted between paired devices. Carryover does not operate a hosted clipboard service, and the apps do not send your clips to the developer.

The iPhone and iPad apps sync when opened or when you use a send or fetch control; they do not continuously read your clipboard in the background. Desktop agents watch for supported clipboard changes while syncing is enabled. Only pair devices you trust: anyone with your group invite or pairing code can join and receive the group's clips.

What stays on each device

Each app keeps settings, a device identifier, known devices and connection information locally. Recent history keeps up to 20 sent or received clips, including text and images, on that device until items are deleted or replaced by newer clips. The latest clip may also remain available in a running desktop agent for a paired device to retrieve.

On iPhone and iPad, the group secret is stored in the system Keychain and is restricted to that device. Local app files may be included in device backups according to your operating system and backup settings. The system clipboard is managed separately by the operating system, and features such as Apple's Universal Clipboard may also share clipboard content under your system settings.

Permissions

  • Local network: finds and connects to your computers on the same network.
  • Clipboard access: reads text or images for syncing and writes received items. iOS may request permission to paste from another app. You can also use the app's Paste to Send control.
  • Camera: optionally scans a pairing QR code. Camera images are processed on the device for scanning; Carryover does not save or upload camera footage. You can paste an invite instead.

You can change camera, local network and paste permissions in system Settings. Denying a permission may prevent the feature that needs it from working.

No advertising or tracking

The apps contain no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Carryover does not sell your data, track you across other apps or websites, or require an account. Operating systems and app stores may separately process installation, purchase or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies and your settings.

Your controls and deletion

Delete an individual Recent item, or use Clear Recent, to remove that device's saved history. On iPhone and iPad, Settings → Leave Group removes that app's saved group secret, known devices, connection addresses and recent history. It does not erase copies already received by another device or clear the system clipboard. Clear those separately on each device.

Removing an app normally removes its local app files, but iOS Keychain items can survive uninstalling. Use Leave Group before uninstalling to remove Carryover's saved pairing secret. If a pairing code is exposed, create a new group and re-pair only your trusted devices. The developer cannot remotely read or delete data stored on your devices.

This website and support

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or local storage. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes connection information, including visitors' IP addresses, to operate and secure the hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement. Following links to GitHub releases, Apple or other services is subject to those services' policies.

If you email support, your email provider and ours process your address, message and any attachments. We use that information to respond and resolve your request, and retain the correspondence only as needed for support and any applicable obligations. Do not send clipboard contents, pairing codes or other secrets. You can email us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any required retention.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date. For questions or requests about information you have sent to support, contact determantechhelp@gmail.com.
